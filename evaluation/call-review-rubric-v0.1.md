# St. Mary's Call Review Rubric v0.1

Date: 2026-09-18  
Total: 100 points

Use this rubric for human QA and future AI-assisted call analysis. The score is a coaching tool, not a substitute for conversion/outcome data.

## 1. Compliance & integrity — 15 points

### Full-credit behaviour
- Identifies St. Mary's and the reason for calling truthfully.
- Uses only accurate/current claims.
- Does not hide material information when directly asked.
- Respects opt-out/no/later requests and privacy.
- No false scarcity, fear, guarantee or deceptive familiarity.

### Critical failures
Any hard flag below requires manager review regardless of numeric score.

---

## 2. Opening & permission — 10 points

### Full-credit behaviour
- Concise introduction.
- Clear relevance/reason for call.
- Checks whether it is an appropriate time when needed.
- Does not ramble into a full pitch immediately.
- Transitions naturally into a relevance/discovery question.

### Common misses
- robotic script;
- vague identity;
- long institutional introduction;
- fake "old friend" style opener;
- continuing despite obvious time resistance.

---

## 3. Discovery & relevance — 20 points

### Full-credit behaviour
- Understands the goal/programme/stage.
- Finds the important decision criteria.
- Clarifies meaningful concerns instead of assuming.
- Understands timing/decision context where relevant.
- Uses open and targeted questions naturally.
- Does not interrogate or ask irrelevant questions merely to increase question count.

### Reviewer test
At the end of the call, can we answer: **Why might this family choose or not choose St. Mary's?**

If not, discovery was probably weak.

---

## 4. Listening & reflection — 15 points

### Full-credit behaviour
- Allows the prospect to finish important thoughts.
- Picks up on what they actually said.
- Reflects/paraphrases/summarizes key needs.
- Corrects understanding when the parent disagrees.
- Changes the conversation based on new information.

### Common misses
- interrupting;
- asking the next scripted question without responding to the answer;
- repeating information already provided;
- pretending to empathize without addressing the actual concern.

---

## 5. Recommendation & value mapping — 15 points

### Full-credit behaviour
- Connects St. Mary's facts directly to the stated need.
- Selects relevant proof rather than dumping all features.
- Explains why the feature matters to this family.
- Uses only approved/current information.
- Recognizes when there is insufficient fit rather than forcing a pitch.

### Common misses
- generic brochure recitation;
- irrelevant achievements;
- too many features;
- unsupported claims;
- recommendation before discovery.

---

## 6. Objection handling — 10 points

Evaluate against **ACAN**:

1. **Acknowledge** — did the agent show they heard it?
2. **Clarify** — did they identify the real objection where clarification was needed?
3. **Answer** — did they answer directly and factually?
4. **Next step/check** — did they check whether the concern was resolved or move appropriately?

### Common misses
- immediate rebuttal;
- arguing;
- clever comeback without understanding;
- answering a different objection;
- endlessly questioning instead of answering;
- pressure after the concern remains unresolved.

---

## 7. Next step / close — 10 points

### Full-credit behaviour
- Chooses a next step appropriate to the lead stage.
- Makes it specific when possible.
- Confirms timing/date/owner where appropriate.
- Does not manufacture urgency.
- Respectfully closes when there is no fit or the person declines.

### Common misses
- "Okay, we'll call you sometime";
- sending a brochure with no agreed follow-up;
- trying to close admission on a clearly early-stage cold call;
- repeated ask after a clear no.

---

## 8. CRM & follow-up discipline — 5 points

This may require CRM data in addition to call audio/transcript.

### Full-credit behaviour
- outcome recorded;
- key need/objection recorded;
- next action recorded;
- follow-up date/time recorded where applicable;
- opt-out/no-call status recorded when applicable.

---

# Interpretation bands

Bands are for coaching triage, not employment decisions.

- **90–100:** strong execution; look for small refinements and consistency.
- **75–89:** broadly effective; coach the highest-impact gaps.
- **60–74:** inconsistent; needs targeted coaching and repeat review.
- **Below 60:** process breakdown; review fundamentals and manager-supervised practice.

A call with a hard flag is **manager review required** even if its numeric score is high.

---

# Hard flags

- deceptive familiarity / misleading identity;
- unsupported result, ranking, scholarship, guarantee, deadline or scarcity claim;
- shaming, fear pressure or coercion;
- pressure to spend/borrow beyond a stated affordability concern;
- ignoring a clear opt-out / do-not-call request;
- disclosing or mishandling private student/family information;
- abusive/disrespectful language;
- materially misleading competitor claim.

---

# Exploratory analytics — track, do not score blindly

The system may calculate:

- agent talk percentage;
- prospect talk percentage;
- longest uninterrupted agent monologue;
- interruption count;
- total questions;
- open questions;
- discovery questions before first recommendation;
- call duration;
- next-step set yes/no;
- scheduled next-step yes/no;
- objection types;
- opt-out/complaint rate;
- stage conversion.

Do **not** impose generic internet benchmarks such as a fixed 43/57 talk ratio, 11–14 questions, or a universal 30-second opener. Those numbers come from other selling environments and must first be compared with St. Mary's own calls by funnel stage.

---

# Recommended review output

Every reviewed call should produce:

1. overall score and component scores;
2. 2–3 things the agent did well;
3. the 1–2 highest-priority coaching misses;
4. exact transcript/timestamp evidence;
5. relevant principle IDs;
6. a better alternative line/behaviour;
7. one role-play drill;
8. whether manager review is required;
9. outcome/next-step status;
10. analytics fields for aggregate trend analysis.

The purpose is to create measurable behavioural improvement, not merely to grade people.
